# Third-party assets

## Cartoon Detective Pack

- Author: Hugues Laborde
- Source: https://hugues-laborde.itch.io/cartoon-detective-pack
- Original format: 74 frames on 64x64 canvases.
- Permission stated by the author on the asset page: the asset may be used in commercial or non-commercial projects.
- Imported into HorrorPlay: original `Idle` (4 frames) and `Run` (8 frames) PNGs.
- HorrorPlay keeps the original authored pixels for the canonical Inspector locomotion; scaling is nearest-neighbor and world lighting/post-processing is applied by Godot.

Credit retained here even though the source page does not state an attribution requirement.
