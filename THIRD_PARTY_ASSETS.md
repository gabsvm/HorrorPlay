# Third-party assets & Licensing Registry

This document records the provenance, author, source, license terms, and derivative treatment of all external asset packs used in HorrorPlay.

## 1. Cartoon Detective Pack
- **Author**: Hugues Laborde
- **Source**: https://hugues-laborde.itch.io/cartoon-detective-pack
- **Original format**: 74 frames on 64x64 canvases.
- **License**: Free for commercial and non-commercial projects.
- **Imported assets**: `assets/third_party/cartoon_detective_pack/` (`Idle` 4 frames, `Run` 8 frames).
- **Usage**: Canonical playable Inspector locomotion and baseline character art. Scaled nearest-neighbor and shaded via Godot runtime lighting.

---

## 2. Gothicvania Town (Streets — Dominant Pack)
- **Author**: Luis Zuno (ansimuz)
- **Source**: https://ansimuz.itch.io/gothicvania-town / https://opengameart.org/content/gothicvania-town
- **License**: Creative Commons Zero (CC0) v1.0 Universal (Public Domain).
- **Raw storage**: `assets/third_party/gothicvania_town/`
- **Derivatives storage**: `assets/images/backgrounds/`, `assets/images/props/`
